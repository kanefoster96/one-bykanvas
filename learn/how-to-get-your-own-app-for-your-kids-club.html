How to get your own app for your kids’ club

Your club’s name in the App Store and Google Play, without paying an agency thousands. The three ways to do it, what Apple and Google need from you, and how long it takes.

Kane Foster, founder of Kanvas One and owner of Kanvas Academy in Newcastle
6 min read · Updated 18 November 2026

A club app used to mean paying an agency thousands of pounds and waiting months. It doesn’t have to now. Here’s how clubs actually get one, what Apple and Google need from you, and the questions to ask before you choose.

First: what “your own app” means

Lots of booking systems say they have an app. Usually that’s their app, with your club listed inside it next to other clubs. Parents download the booking company’s app, not yours.

Your own app is in the App Store and Google Play under your club’s name, with your logo on the parent’s home screen. When you send a notification, it comes from your club. That’s what makes a small club look established, and it’s why parents open it.

The three ways to get one

  1. Have one built from scratch. An agency designs and codes it for you. You get exactly what you ask for, but it usually costs thousands up front, takes months, and you pay again every time Apple or Google change something.
  2. Use an app builder. You drag and drop the screens yourself. Cheaper, but you’re the one building it, and most builders aren’t made for clubs: no registers, no trial bookings, no memberships that take payment every month.
  3. Use a service that builds it for clubs. The club features are already made (bookings, payments, registers, messages), and your app is set up with your name, logo and timetable. This is the quickest and usually the cheapest way. It’s how Kanvas One works.

What Apple and Google need from you

Whichever way you choose, your app is published under your own developer accounts, so it carries your club’s name. Here’s what that involves:

  • An Apple Developer account: about £79 a year. To show your club’s name rather than your own, you sign up as an organisation, which needs a free D-U-N-S number for your business.
  • A Google Play account: a one-off $25. An organisation account also needs the D-U-N-S number, and it skips the rule that new personal accounts must test with 12 people for 14 days before going live.
  • App review: Apple and Google check every app before it goes live, usually 1 to 4 weeks.

The paperwork is where most club owners get stuck, not the app. Whoever builds your app should set these accounts up with you.

What your app should do

  1. Let parents book a trial, join and pay monthly by card in a couple of taps.
  2. Give every child a pass to scan in at the door, so registers fill themselves in.
  3. Send free notifications to one class, one age group or everyone.
  4. Chase failed payments for you, and let parents pause or change their plan.
  5. Show their child’s progress, levels and badges, so families have a reason to stay.
  6. Work on your website too, for parents who never download it.

Questions to ask before you choose

  • Is it in the stores under my club’s name, or theirs?
  • Do you take a percentage of my payments?
  • Will you set up my Apple and Google accounts with me?
  • Who moves my current members across?
  • If I leave, do I keep my members’ details and my web address?

Questions

How much does it cost to get an app for my club?

Built from scratch by an agency, usually thousands of pounds up front plus upkeep. With a service made for clubs, it’s a monthly fee: Kanvas One is £349 a month with your app, website, bookings, payments and ads included. On top, Apple charges about £79 a year and Google a one-off $25.

How long does it take to get my club app in the App Store?

Once your developer accounts are ready, Apple and Google review the app, usually in 1 to 4 weeks. Your website, with the same bookings and payments, can be live in about a week while you wait.

Do I need a D-U-N-S number?

To publish under your club’s name rather than your own, yes. It’s free, and whoever builds your app should help you get it.

Can my app have my club’s name and logo?

It should. If parents download an app with another company’s name on it and find your club inside, that’s their app, not yours.
