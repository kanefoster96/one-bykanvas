No. See your free page first. Then Starter is the site for £25, Business adds bookings and payments for £25 more, and Max adds the ads and the ranking work. Move between them any month.

Your call, and you can change it any time. Below about £10 a day there is not enough to learn from, so that is where I suggest starting. It is paid by you, directly to Meta, on your own ad account.
