Questions. Answered straight.

Why is it £250 a month?

It replaces your website, job and quoting software, follow-up texts and emails, a business phone with missed-call text back, live chat, an agency to run your ads, and someone to get you found on Google and AI search. Bought separately that’s £530 to £1,740+ a month. At £180 a job, two extra jobs a month cover it.

What if the ads don’t work for me?

That’s our risk, not yours. If your first 3 months of ads bring fewer than 10 enquiries, your next 3 months of One are free.

What counts as an enquiry?

Someone getting in touch through the system we run for you: a quote request on your website or ad, a call to your business number, or a live chat message. Each person counts once, whether or not it becomes a job. The full detail is in our terms.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Customers book, get their reminders, accept quotes and pay by text and link, with nothing to download. The app is for you and your team. Customers on a service plan can log in to see their plan, their next service and their job history.

Can my engineers use it?

Yes. Each one sees their own jobs for the day, the customer and property details, and the offer for each job, and can send “on my way” and take payment from their phone.

Can I sell service plans?

Yes. Set up a plan such as a yearly service paid monthly, and customers join on your website, from a quote, or on the spot while you’re with them. It’s paid by card every month into your own Stripe account, and we take 0%.

What happens when I miss a call?

They get a text straight away from your business number, with a link to book a free quote and a promise you’ll call back. Most people book from the text instead of ringing the next number.

Who pays for the ads?

Your first 3 months of ads: we do, up to £100 a month, as a free test of what ads can do for you. After that it’s your choice. Keep them going on your own budget, paid straight to , and we keep running them for you at no extra cost. Or turn them off. We pause the ads when your diary is full.

How long until it’s ready?

Your website is usually finished within a week, with a preview of your app, and we start moving your customers across as soon as it’s live. Your app goes to Apple and Google as soon as your developer accounts are ready. Their review takes 1 to 4 weeks, and until then every app feature works on your website. Once both are live, usually 4 to 8 weeks after you join, your ads start.

Do we get our own app?

Yes, with your name and logo, in the App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google charge for their developer accounts (£79 a year and a one-off $25), paid to them, not us.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s monthly, and you can cancel any time from your account.

What kind of businesses is it for?

Trades and home services that quote, visit and get paid: heating engineers, plumbers, electricians, roofers, builders, locksmiths, cleaners and gardeners. If you work a different way, tell me and I’ll set it up around how you do business.

Is our data ours?

Yes. Your customers, prices and data are only ever yours. Never shared, never used by anyone else.
