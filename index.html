Questions. Answered straight.

Why is it £250 a month?

Because it replaces the lot: your website, booking and membership software, text and email follow-ups, a business phone, an online shop and an agency to run your ads. Bought separately that’s £410 to £1,350+ a month, plus £1,000+ to build a website. And it pays for itself quickly: at £50 a month, 5 new members cover it.

What if the ads don’t work for me?

That’s our risk, not yours. If your first 3 months of ads bring fewer than 10 trial bookings, your next 3 months of One are free. Every area is different, so we keep testing and improving your ads, using what works in our own academy.

What if nobody joins after the ads?

Months 2 to 4 of ads are on us, so you haven’t spent a penny on them. From month 5, if we fund your ads and nobody new joins, you pay nothing for them. And every trial gets reminders and a follow-up with the link to join: the step most owners never have time for.

Who pays for the ads?

For months 2, 3 and 4, we do, up to £150 a month. Month 1 is spent building and testing everything, so the ads land on a website and app that work. After that, you choose. We keep paying, and each new member pays us their first membership payment, then everything they pay is yours (for invoiced or one-off fees, it’s 20% of their first payment). Or you pay Meta yourself and keep every fee. Switch any time: tell us and we swap straight away.

Can I turn the ads off?

Yes, any time. Just want the system? Tell us and we switch the ads off, and you keep everything else for the same £250. If a class is full, we pause its ads anyway, so you never pay to find people you can’t take.

How long until it’s ready?

Your website is ready within 14 days, and members can install your app from your website straight away. The App Store and Google Play versions follow within up to 8 weeks, as Apple and Google review them. Your ads start in month 2, once everything is tested.

How easy is it to set up?

You fill in a short form in your account about your classes, prices and timetable, and send a few photos. We do the rest: building your website and app, setting up payments, and moving your members across.

We already use booking software. Can we switch?

Yes. We move your members and classes across for you, and help them move their payments over, so you’re not starting from scratch.

Do we get our own app?

Yes, with your name and logo. From day one, members install it from your website onto their phone, and we publish it to the App Store and Google Play for you. Apple and Google charge for their developer accounts: £79 a year and a one-off $25, paid to them, not us.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s monthly, and you can cancel any time from your account. You stay because it’s working, not because you’re tied in.

What kind of businesses is it for?

Clubs, classes and in-person services where people pay every month: kids’ clubs, dance and cheer schools, gymnastics, martial arts, swim schools, gyms and personal trainers. If your members pay you monthly, it’s built for you.

Is our data ours?

Yes. Your members, prices and data are only ever yours. Never shared, never used by anyone else.
