Is the build really free?

Yes. No setup fee, no build fee. Your first month puts you live the same day. If you upgrade to Business, your features follow within 14 days, or the next month is free.

What if I don’t like it?

You see your page within 24 hours, before you pay. Don’t love it? You owe nothing. After you join, edits are included, as many as you like.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, a link to you online, and your email. Photos and prices later, if you have them. If not, we’ll sort that.

What does a change cost once I'm live?

Nothing. Ask for edits whenever you need them, as many as you like, and we work through them in turn. On Business they’re done within 48 hours. Live tools like a menu or your prices are yours to change instantly.

What happens if I cancel?

No exit fee, no further payments. Your site stays up to the end of the month you paid for, and your domain transfers to you.
