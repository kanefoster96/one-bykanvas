Questions. Answered straight.

Why is it £250 a month?

It replaces your website, booking and membership software, follow-up texts and emails, a business phone, a shop and an agency to run your ads. Bought separately that’s £410 to £1,350+ a month. At £50 a month, 5 new members cover it.

What if the ads don’t work for me?

That’s our risk, not yours. If your first 3 months of ads bring fewer than 10 trial bookings, your next 3 months of One are free.

What if nobody joins after the ads?

Months 2 to 4 of ads are on us, so you’ve spent nothing on them. After that, if we fund your ads and nobody new joins, you pay nothing for them.

Who pays for the ads?

Months 2 to 4: we do, up to £100 a month. After that, you choose. We keep paying, up to £100 a month, and take each new member’s first payment, or you pay for your own ads and keep every fee. Switch any time. The full detail is in our terms.

Can I turn the ads off?

Yes, any time, and keep everything else for the same £250. Full classes have their ads paused anyway.

How long until it’s ready?

Your website in 7 to 14 days, and we start moving your members across as soon as it’s live. Your app goes to Apple and Google as soon as your developer accounts are ready. Their review takes 1 to 4 weeks, and until then every app feature works on your website. Once it’s all ready, usually from month 2, your ads start.

How easy is it to set up?

Fill in a short form about your classes, prices and timetable, and send a few photos. We do the rest, including moving your members across.

We already use booking software. Can we switch?

Yes. We move your members and classes across for you, and help them move their payments over.

Do we get our own app?

Yes, with your name and logo, in the App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google charge for their developer accounts (£79 a year and a one-off $25), paid to them, not us.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s monthly, and you can cancel any time from your account.

What kind of businesses is it for?

Clubs, classes and in-person services where people pay monthly: kids’ clubs, dance and cheer schools, gymnastics, martial arts, swim schools, gyms and personal trainers.

Is our data ours?

Yes. Your members, prices and data are only ever yours. Never shared, never used by anyone else.
