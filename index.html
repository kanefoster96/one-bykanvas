Fair questions. Straight answers.

We already use booking software. Do we have to switch everything at once?

No. New families can join through Kanvas One from day one while your current members stay where they are. When you’re ready, we move your members, memberships and timetable across for you, ideally between terms, and your parents get one message with the link to your new app.

Will parents have to put their card details in again?

Card details can’t always be moved from one payment system to another, so most families add theirs once, in your new app. We send each family the link and answer their questions, so you don’t have to. New families just join.

What if we want to leave?

Cancel any time from your account. Your web address, your Stripe account (it’s in your name) and your members’ details are yours: we send you an export of your data and move your web address to you.

You run a dance school. Isn’t that a conflict?

It’s why Kanvas One works: it runs our own classes every week. We only take one club per activity in each area, never one near our own, and your members and numbers are only ever used to run your club.
