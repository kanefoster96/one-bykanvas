Questions.

Is the build really free?

Yes. There's no setup fee and no build fee — the monthly price covers all of it. Your first month is paid when you sign up, which is what gets us started; you'll see a draft within a few days and be online within 7 days. Cancel anytime.

What if I don’t like it?

You see a real example page before you pay anything. If you don’t love it, you owe nothing and nobody chases you. Once you’ve joined, every change is included — ask, and it’s done within 48 hours.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, what you do, any photos or logos you have, and a rough idea of what the site should do. Five minutes on your phone. If you don't have photos or copy, we'll sort that too.

What does a change cost once I'm live?

Nothing. Edits and new features are included, however big — bookings, payments, a members area, a new page. Anything we build you as a live tool, like a menu or your prices, is yours to change instantly; everything else, just ask.

Do I have to write the Keep Book emails?

No. They’re written for you and sent in your name. You add the customer after the job, and the reminder and the note go out on their own. It’s all within UK rules for emailing your own customers, with an opt-out on every one.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel anytime and that's it — no exit fee. No further payments are taken, and your site stays up until the month you've paid for ends. We'll transfer your domain to you and help you move your content wherever you're going.
