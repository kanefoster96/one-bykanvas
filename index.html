Questions.

Who pays for the ads?

For your first 3 months, we do. After that you choose: pay Meta yourself and keep every fee, or we keep paying and each new member from the ads pays their first month to us. If no one joins from the ads, you pay nothing for them.

How do you know which members came from the ads?

Every trial booked from an ad is tagged as it’s booked. You see it in your dashboard: the ad, the trial, and whether they joined. Members who find you through Google or a friend are always yours.

What if our classes are full?

We pause the ads for full classes, so you never pay to find families you can’t take. You just pay the £249 for the system, and we turn the ads back on when you have room.

Do we get our own app?

Yes. From day one, members install your club’s app from your website onto their phone. Want it in the App Store and Google Play too? We build, publish and update it for you. Apple and Google charge for the developer accounts (about £79 a year and £20 once), paid in your club’s name, because the app is yours.

We already use booking software. Can we switch?

Yes. We move your members and classes across for you, and help parents move their payments over, so you’re not starting from scratch.

Is there a contract?

A 3-month minimum, then monthly. If we don’t get you at least 10 trial bookings in your first 3 months, you can leave whenever you like.

What kind of businesses is it for?

Businesses that run on classes and memberships: kids’ clubs, dance and cheer schools, gymnastics, martial arts, swim schools, gyms, yoga and Pilates studios, music schools and tutors.
