Not sure it’s right for your club? Let’s chat first.

Questions. Answered straight.

Why is it £250 a month?

It replaces your website, booking and membership software, follow-up texts and emails, a business phone, a shop, an agency to run your ads, and someone to get you found on Google and AI search. Bought separately that’s £560 to £1,850+ a month. At £50 a month, 5 new members cover it.

Do you take a cut of our payments?

No. Members pay into your own Stripe account, at Stripe’s own card rates, paid to Stripe. We take 0%, so your bill stays £250 however many members you have.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s monthly, and you can cancel any time from your account.

We already use booking software. Can we switch?

Yes, and between terms is the easiest time. We move your members, memberships and timetable across for you, and your parents get one message with the link to your new app.

Will we be left on our own after the switch-over?

No. Moving you across is the start, not the end. You keep messaging me, a real person who runs a club too, for as long as you’re with us, and you get a report every month.

What if the ads don’t work for me?

You’ll see exactly what they brought in. Your first 3 months of ads are on us, up to £100 a month, as a launch boost when your app and website go live, so finding out costs you nothing. After that, keep them going on your own budget, or switch them off and keep everything else at the same price.

How long until it’s ready?

Your website is usually finished within a week, with a preview of your app, and we start moving your members across as soon as it’s live. Your app goes to Apple and Google as soon as your developer accounts are ready. Their review takes 1 to 4 weeks, and until then every app feature works on your website. Once both are live, usually 4 to 8 weeks after you join, your ads start.

Do we get our own app?

Yes, with your name and logo, in the App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google charge for their developer accounts (£79 a year and a one-off $25), paid to them, not us.

What happens after the 3 months of ads?

You’ve seen what ads can do for your club, on us. Want more? Set a monthly budget, paid straight to from your own , and we keep running your ads as part of One: pausing the ones that tire, refreshing the , and testing new ads every month. Or stop them and keep everything else for the same £250. We never take a cut of what your members pay.

Who pays for the ads?

Your first 3 months of ads: we do, up to £100 a month, as a free test of what ads can do for you. After that it’s your choice. Keep them going on your own budget, paid straight to , and we keep running them for you at no extra cost. Or turn them off. The full detail is in our terms.

Will parents find us on Google and AI search?

That’s part of One. Your site is built so Google and AI search, like ChatGPT and Google’s AI answers, understand your classes, ages, times and area: a page for each class, your kept up to date, reviews asked for after every trial, and clear answers to the questions parents ask. This is called and , and it’s done for you. Our own academy gets trial bookings from Google and AI search, not just ads. No one honest can promise a ranking, so we don’t.

Why don’t we show up on Google or AI already?

Usually because nothing tells them enough about you: a website that doesn’t spell out your classes, ages, times and area, a Google profile that’s half filled in, and too few recent reviews. Google and AI recommend the clubs they understand and trust, and most clubs never get that far. We fix all of it and keep it up to date every month, as part of the £250.

Can I turn the ads off?

Yes, any time, and keep everything else for the same £250. Full classes have their ads paused anyway.

How easy is it to set up?

Fill in a short form about your classes, prices and timetable, and send a few photos. We do the rest, including moving your members across.

Our software mostly works. Should we switch?

Only if something is actually broken. Tell me what isn’t working, whether it’s the fees, a missing feature, or parents and staff finding it confusing, and I’ll tell you straight if One fixes it. If it doesn’t, I’ll say so.

Will our coaches and parents find it easy?

Coaches open the app and the class that’s on is waiting: tap to check everyone in, or scan a QR code, and if a payment hasn’t gone through it tells them what to do. Parents see their child’s photo, level, upcoming classes and the placements they’ve won, with their class pass to scan in. Look round your app for free and try both sides.

Can we charge by the term?

Yes. Tell us your term dates and every member gets their invoice on the first day of term, paid by card in the app, with reminders if it isn’t paid. Or monthly, or a mix. We set it up the way your club already charges.

Do texts cost extra?

No. The texts we send for you are included: trial reminders, payment reminders, and a text when you miss a call, with a link to chat or book. Parents can call your business number in your opening hours; out of hours they reach your voicemail and get the same text. To call or message parents yourself, you use WhatsApp Business on your business number, which is free. Live chat, push notifications, app messages and email are free and unlimited too.

What if we need something One doesn’t do?

Tell me. If it would make your club easier to run, I’ll do my best to build it into your app and website, at no extra cost.

What kind of businesses is it for?

Clubs, classes and in-person services where people pay monthly: kids’ clubs, dance and cheer schools, gymnastics, martial arts, swim schools, gyms and personal trainers.

Is our data ours?

Yes. Your members, prices and data are only ever yours. Never shared, never used by anyone else.
