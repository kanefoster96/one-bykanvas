Questions.

Is the build really free?

Yes. There's no setup fee and no build fee — the £50 monthly price covers all of it. Your first month is paid when you sign up, which is what gets us started; you'll see a draft within a few days and be online within 10 days. Cancel anytime.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, what you do, any photos or logos you have, and a rough idea of what the site should do. If you don't have photos or copy, we'll sort that too.

Who owns the domain?

We register it and look after it while you’re with us, so there’s no renewal to remember and nothing to set up. If you ever leave, we transfer it to you — it’s yours to take, at no cost.

What does a change cost once I'm live?

Nothing. Edits and new features are included on every plan — ask, and it joins the queue. We work through requests one at a time, Max plans first, then Business. Max exists for the growth work and the extras, not because Business is missing anything.

Can I update things myself?

Yes — whatever we build you as a live tool, like a menu, your prices or a class timetable, is yours to change as often as you like, instantly. For design changes — a new homepage photo, new pictures, how a page looks — just ask us: they’re included too, and we’ll pick them up in your plan’s turn.

What if I need a feature that isn't listed?

Ask. Bookings, payments, loyalty schemes, stock lists, customer accounts — anything you ask for is included, however big, during the build and after it. Later requests join the queue and get built in your plan’s turn.

Can I change plan later?

Any time, up or down. Changes apply from your next monthly payment.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel anytime and that's it — no exit fee. No further payments are taken, and your site stays up until the month you've paid for ends. We'll transfer your domain to you and help you move your content wherever you're going.
