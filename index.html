Is the build really free?

Yes. No setup fee, no build fee. Join for £9.99 a month and you’re live within 24 hours. If you upgrade to Business, your features follow within 14 days, or the next month is free.

What if I don’t like it?

Tell us and we change it. Changes to your words and photos are included, as many as you like, and there’s no contract: cancel any month.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, a link to you online, and your email. Photos and prices later, if you have them. If not, we’ll sort that.

Nothing. Ask for changes to your words and photos whenever you need them, as many as you like, and we work through them in turn. On Business they’re done within 48 hours. Live tools like a menu or your prices are yours to change instantly.

What happens if I cancel?

No exit fee, no further payments. Your site stays up to the end of the month you paid for, and your domain transfers to you.
