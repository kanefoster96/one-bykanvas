Questions.

Is the build really free?

Yes. There's no setup fee and no build fee — the £50 monthly price covers all of it. Your first month is paid when you sign up, which is what gets us started; you'll usually see a draft within two to five days and be live inside ten. Cancel any time with a month's notice.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, what you do, any photos or logos you have, and a rough idea of what the site should do. If you don't have photos or copy, we'll sort that too.

Who owns the domain?

You do. It's registered for your business and if you ever leave, it goes with you.

What counts as an edit request?

Any change to your live site: swapping photos, rewriting a section, adding a page, updating prices, changing opening hours. On Business they're £35 each. Pro includes 5 a month, Max includes 10.

Can I update things myself?

Yes — anything we set up as live content, like menus, prices, opening hours or gallery images, you can edit yourself at any time. That never counts as an edit request.

What if I need a feature that isn't listed?

Ask. Bookings, payments, loyalty schemes, stock lists, customer accounts — if it can be built, it gets built into your plan.

Can I change plan later?

Any time, up or down. Changes apply from your next monthly payment.

What happens if I cancel?

Give a month's notice and that's it — no exit fee. We'll transfer your domain to you and help you move your content wherever you're going.
