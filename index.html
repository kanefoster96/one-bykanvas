Questions.

Is the setup really free?

Yes. No setup fee, no deposit, nothing upfront. Your first monthly payment happens when your site goes live, and it covers the build, the domain and the hosting.

How quickly can I be online?

Within two weeks of you sending the form back. Most sites are ready sooner — the two weeks is the outside limit, not the target.

How much work is it for me?

One short form, about five minutes. Screenshots of your menu or services, your hours, your address and any photos you'd like used. If you're missing something, tell us on the form and we'll handle it.

Who owns the domain?

You do. It's registered for your business, and if you ever leave it goes with you.

What counts as an edit request?

Any change to your live site: swapping photos, rewriting a section, adding a page, changing prices. On Business they're £35 each. Pro includes 5 a month, Max includes 10. Anything set up as live content — menus, hours, galleries — you change yourself for free.

Can I get the app without a website?

Yes. The app is £99 a month on its own, and that includes building it, publishing it to both stores, bug fixes and one improvement request a month. Plenty of businesses only need one or the other — take whichever fits.

What if I need a feature that isn't listed?

Ask. Bookings, payments, loyalty schemes, stock lists, customer accounts — if it can be built, it gets built into your plan at no extra cost.

Can I change plan later?

Any time, up or down. Changes apply from your next monthly payment.

What happens if I cancel?

Give a month's notice and that's it — no exit fee. We'll transfer the domain to you and help move your content wherever you're going.
