I’m on a tutoring directory already.

I’m fully booked. Why would I need a site?

To stay that way. A waiting list fills September in July, and a page that ranks means you choose the students rather than the other way round. It also lets you raise rates.

No. Bank transfer works. Online payment just means blocks are paid before the first lesson.

Yes. Grades improved, first names or initials, and quotes from parents with their permission.
