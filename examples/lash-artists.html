How long do extensions last?

With infills every two to three weeks, as long as you like. A set on its own lasts three to four weeks.

Will they damage my natural lashes?

Not when they’re applied properly. We only use weights your lashes can carry.

What should I do before my appointment?

Come with clean, mascara-free lashes. That’s it.

Can I wear them if I wear contacts?

Yes. Just take them out for the appointment itself.
