Questions about plans.

Which plan should I start on?

Max if you want new customers finding you. Business if you just want to be online. Starter if you love your free page as it is and want to be found and called. Move between them any month.

What’s the difference between an edit and a feature?

An edit changes what’s there: photos, wording, prices, hours. A feature is built new: bookings, payments, a members area. Both included on every plan.

Can I start on Max and drop back to Business later?

Yes, any month. The ranking work stays done. The texts to your customers stop, unless you ask to keep them as an add-on.

Is it really unlimited?

Yes. Requests are done one at a time, Max first. A long list takes longer; it never costs more.

Can I have email at my own address on Business?

Set up for you on Max. On Business, add Google Workspace yourself and I’ll point your domain at it.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly, cancel anytime, and your web address transfers to you when you go.

What happens if I cancel?

No exit fee. Your domain transfers to you and I help you move your content.
