Questions about plans.

Which plan should I start on?

Starter, if you want to be found, called and messaged. Business, if you want bookings, payments or live chat. Max, if you want the whole thing run for you: it is built per business type, so pick yours. Move between them any month.

What do I get in my first month?

Paying monthly, Starter is £9.99 from day one, and Business and Max are half price for the first month. Paying for the year, you get two months free and the Launch Boost: your first month spent getting you found on Google. One or the other, not both.

What’s the difference between an edit and a feature?

An edit changes what’s there: photos, wording, prices, hours. A feature is built new: bookings, payments, a members area. Starter gets an edit a month. Business and Max get both, unlimited.

How do the ads on Max work?

You set a monthly budget, paid by you straight to Meta, and change it whenever you like. £150 to £300 for the first month is enough to learn what works. We write the offer, build the ad and the form on your site, run it, and send you a note each month saying what it cost and what came in. We need a photo or a line of text from you, and access to your Facebook page.

Can I start on Max and drop back to Business later?

Yes, any month. The ranking work stays done. The ads and the follow-up emails stop. If Max is full when you want back in, you wait for a place.

Is it really unlimited?

On Business and Max, yes. Requests are done one at a time, Max first. A long list takes longer; it never costs more.

Can I have email at my own address on Business?

Set up for you on Max. On Business, add Google Workspace yourself and we’ll point your domain at it.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly, cancel anytime, and your web address transfers to you when you go.

What happens if I cancel?

No exit fee. Your domain transfers to you and we help you move your content.
