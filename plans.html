Questions about plans.

Which plan should I start on?

Business. It already includes one point a month, which covers most sites without you needing to think about it. If you find yourself asking for more than that regularly, move up — it takes one click and starts from your next payment.

What’s the difference between an edit and a feature?

An edit changes what’s already there — new photos, new wording, new prices, new opening hours. A feature is something new that has to be built, like bookings, payments or a members area. An edit costs one point, a feature three, on any plan — Business includes one point a month, Pro three, Max five. Ask for more than your plan covers and it’s £40 for an edit or £120 for a feature.

Do unused points roll over?

No. Points reset each month, so it’s worth matching your plan to how often you actually change things. If you go over, the extra is charged at the normal rate — £40 an edit, £120 a feature.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s monthly, and you cancel with a month’s notice. We transfer your web address to you when you go, so nothing is held over you.

What happens if I cancel?

We transfer the domain to you and help you move your content wherever you’re going. No exit fee, no holding anything hostage.
