Questions about plans.

Which plan should I start on?

Business. Everything is included there — the site, and unlimited changes made by us. Max is for when you want the site actively grown: we improve it every month without being asked, work on your search ranking, and add business email at your own address, with your requests first in the queue. Move between them in one click, any time.

What’s the difference between an edit and a feature?

An edit changes what’s already there — new photos, new wording, new prices, new opening hours. A feature is something new that has to be built, like bookings, payments or a members area. Both are included on every plan, before and after launch — the label only affects how long the work takes.

Can I start on Max and drop back to Business later?

Yes, any month. Most of Max’s value is the ranking work, and that stays done when you step down — the pages, the speed, the details Google has already read. The texts to your customers stop on Business, unless you message us before you switch and we keep them on as a small monthly add-on. Nothing to pay to move, and you keep Max until your month runs out.

Is it really unlimited?

Yes — ask for as much as you like, and there is never an extra bill for asking. We do requests one site at a time, Max plans first, then Business. A long list just takes longer to work through; it never costs more.

What is KeepBook, and do I have to write the emails?

KeepBook turns a one-off job into a customer who comes back. After a job you add them on your phone, and KeepBook reminds them when the next one is due, with a Book button, and sends a short note from you every three months. The emails are written for you and sent in your name; you can change any of them. It’s all within UK rules for emailing your own customers, with an opt-out on every one. Included on Business and Max.

Can I have email at my own address on Business?

An inbox at your own address (you@yourbusiness.co.uk) comes set up for you on Max. On Business you can add Google Workspace yourself, and we’ll point your domain at it for you — just ask.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s monthly, and you cancel anytime. We transfer your web address to you when you go, so nothing is held over you.

What happens if I cancel?

We transfer the domain to you and help you move your content wherever you’re going. No exit fee, no holding anything hostage.
