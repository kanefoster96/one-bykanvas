Questions about plans.

Which plan should I start on?

Starter, if you want to be found, called and messaged. Business, if you want bookings, payments or live chat. Max, if you want new customers finding you every month. Move between them any month.

What do I get in my first month?

Paying monthly, your first month is half price, on any plan. Paying for the year, you get two months free and the Launch Boost: your first month spent getting you found on Google. One or the other, not both.

What’s the difference between an edit and a feature?

An edit changes what’s there: photos, wording, prices, hours. A feature is built new: bookings, payments, a members area. Starter gets an edit a month. Business and Max get both, unlimited.

Can I start on Max and drop back to Business later?

Yes, any month. The ranking work stays done. The texts to your customers stop, unless you ask to keep them as an add-on.

Is it really unlimited?

On Business and Max, yes. Requests are done one at a time, Max first. A long list takes longer; it never costs more.

Can I have email at my own address on Business?

Set up for you on Max. On Business, add Google Workspace yourself and I’ll point your domain at it.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly, cancel anytime, and your web address transfers to you when you go.

What happens if I cancel?

No exit fee. Your domain transfers to you and I help you move your content.
