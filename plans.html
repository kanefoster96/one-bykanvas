Questions about plans.

Which plan should I start on?

Business. Most sites don’t need changing often, so paying monthly for changes you may never ask for is money wasted. If you find yourself asking regularly, move up then — it takes one click and starts from your next payment.

What’s the difference between an edit and a feature?

An edit changes what’s already there — new photos, new wording, new prices, new opening hours. A feature is something new that has to be built, like bookings, payments or a members area. Edits are £35, features are £90, and both are included on Pro and Max.

Do unused requests roll over?

No. They reset each month, which is another reason to start on Business and only move up when you know you’ll use them.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s monthly, and you cancel with a month’s notice. We transfer your web address to you when you go, so nothing is held over you.

What happens if I cancel?

We transfer the domain to you and help you move your content wherever you’re going. No exit fee, no holding anything hostage.
