Questions about plans.

Which plan should I start on?

Business. Everything is included there — the site, unlimited changes made by us, email marketing to your customers. Max is for when you want the site actively grown: we improve it every month without being asked, work on your search ranking, and add business email at your own address, with your requests first in the queue. Move between them in one click, any time.

What’s the difference between an edit and a feature?

An edit changes what’s already there — new photos, new wording, new prices, new opening hours. A feature is something new that has to be built, like bookings, payments or a members area. Both are included on every plan, before and after launch — the label only affects how long the work takes.

Is it really unlimited?

Yes — ask for as much as you like, and there is never an extra bill for asking. We do requests one site at a time, Max plans first, then Business. A long list just takes longer to work through; it never costs more.

Can I have email at my own address on Business?

Email marketing is already included on Business. An inbox at your own address (you@yourbusiness.co.uk) comes set up for you on Max; on Business you can add Google Workspace yourself, and we’ll point your domain at it for you — just ask.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s monthly, and you cancel anytime. We transfer your web address to you when you go, so nothing is held over you.

What happens if I cancel?

We transfer the domain to you and help you move your content wherever you’re going. No exit fee, no holding anything hostage.
