Keep Book — on Business and Max

The client who meant to rebook.

Booksy and Treatwell get you found once, then show your client the whole list next time. Keep Book keeps her coming back to your chair.

Remember Sophie, the balayage in March? She loved it, said she’d be back in ten weeks. Ten weeks came and went. Her roots came through, she opened Instagram, an ad for the new place on the high street came up, and that was that.

Nothing was wrong with you. There was just nothing from you.

With Keep Book, Sophie goes in your book after her appointment. When her ten weeks are up, a reminder lands with a button that says Book my next one. Nails and lashes? Infills reminded at two or three weeks. And every few months a short note in your name: still here, and there’s a quiet week coming if you want a slot. She never has to remember to rebook, because you remember for her.

How it works

  3. A short note every three monthsPlain text, in your name, no logo, no newsletter. “Still here if you’re due a refresh, or fancy trying something new. No obligation.” Your name, your number.

Questions.

I already take bookings on Instagram and Facebook.

Keep them. Instagram shows your posts to a fraction of your followers and does not appear when someone searches “hairdresser near me”. Your site does, and it takes the booking while you are with a client.

I use a booking app already. Will this replace it?

It can, or the site can link to the one you have. Either way, your clients book from your own site, where Google sends them.

Can I change prices myself?

Yes, from your dashboard, or message us and we do it for you.

What if I don’t like it?

You see a real example page before you pay anything. If you don’t love it, you owe nothing and nobody chases you.

Do I have to take card payments?

No. Cash and bank transfer work too. Online payment is there if you want it.

How do the bookings and payments actually work?

We set them up with you after you join, the way you already work. Most people tell us in five minutes and it is done.

Do I need Max?

Only if you want to climb Google every month. Business already includes local SEO at launch.

What happens if I cancel?

Your site goes offline, there is no exit fee, and your domain transfers to you free.

Keep Book: do I have to write the emails?

No. They’re written for you, in plain text and in your name, and you can change any of them. Your part is adding the customer after the job — name, email, what you did, the date — about thirty seconds on your phone.

Won’t my customers find it annoying?

One reminder when they’re due, one nudge two weeks later if they haven’t booked, and a two-line note from you every three months. That’s all, and every email has an unsubscribe link. It reads like you keeping in touch, not like marketing.

Is emailing past customers allowed under UK rules?

Yes, for your own customers. UK rules let you email people you’ve done work for about similar work, as long as they can opt out at any time — the “soft opt-in”, which doesn’t need a tick box. Every Keep Book email has an unsubscribe link, and nobody goes in your book unless you’ve done a job for them.
