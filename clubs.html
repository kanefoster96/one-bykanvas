Questions.

Who pays for the ads?

For months 2, 3 and 4, we do, up to £150 a month. Month 1 is spent building and testing everything. After that, we keep paying and each new customer pays us their first subscription payment if they’re on a membership or repeat subscription (monthly, every three months, any interval), or 20% of their first payment if you invoice them or they pay for a one-off service. Only their first payment counts, then everything they pay is yours. Or you pay Meta yourself and keep every fee. Want to pay for your own ads instead? Tell us and we switch straight away, and every new customer from then on is fully yours. If no one new joins, you pay nothing for the ads.

From month 5, which new members count?

Anyone who books a trial through your system and then joins, wherever they found you: an ad, Google or a friend. We’re spending £150 a month growing your club, and the system runs every trial, so it’s one simple rule. You see each one in your dashboard: the trial, when they joined and their first payment. Your existing members are always yours.

What if our classes are full?

We pause the ads for full classes, so you never pay to find families you can’t take. You just pay the £250 for the system, and we turn the ads back on when you have room.

We already use booking software. Can we switch?

Yes. We move your members and classes across for you, and help parents move their payments over, so you’re not starting from scratch.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s monthly, and you can cancel any time from your account.

What kind of clubs is it for?

Kids’ activity clubs that run weekly classes and memberships: dance, cheer, gymnastics, martial arts, swimming, drama, football and multi-sport.

Who owns our members and data?

You do. Your members, your payments and your ad account are yours, and they go with you if you ever leave.
