What about changes later?

Changes after launch are included on every plan — there is no per-change fee and no monthly allowance.

An edit changes what's already there: new photos, new wording, new prices, new hours.

A new feature is something built fresh: bookings, payments, a members area, a whole new page. Both are included — ask, and it joins the queue.

We work through requests one at a time, in plan order. Pro (£120) puts your requests ahead of the Business queue and adds business email at your own address. Max (£250) goes first in every queue, plus monthly work on your ranking.

Everything you ask for while we're building is included, however big. And any live tool we set up for you — a menu, your prices, a timetable — is free to change as often as you want. Design is separate: a new homepage photo or a change to how a page looks is an edit.
