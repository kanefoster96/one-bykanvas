You do, directly, on your own ad account. We never hold or spend your advertising money. The £249 is for the work.

No one honest can promise that. What we promise is the work: a proper offer, a proper ad, a page that converts, the follow-ups, and a note every month showing what it brought in, so you decide with numbers.
