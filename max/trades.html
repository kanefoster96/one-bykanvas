The model.

Questions.

How much should the ad budget be?

Your call, and you can change it any time. £150 to £300 for the first month is enough to learn what works. Below about £10 a day there is not enough to learn from.

Who pays Meta?

You do, directly, on your own ad account. I never hold or spend your advertising money. The £250 is for the work.

Will it definitely bring jobs?

No one honest can promise that. What I promise is the work: a proper offer, a proper ad, a page that converts, the follow-ups, and a note every month showing what it brought in, so you decide with numbers.

What if I already have a website?

I build the new one, move your address across, and nothing goes offline in between. Your reviews and ranking come with you.

Can I start smaller?

Yes. Starter is the site for £25, Business adds bookings and payments for £25 more. Move up to Trades Max any month, if there is a place.

Why only ten businesses?

Because running ads properly takes attention every week. Ten is what I can do well. The first ten pay £250 and keep that price.
