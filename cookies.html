Cookies.

There is no banner on this site, because there is nothing on it that needs your consent.

Last updated 31 August 2026.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Nothing on this site follows you to another one. The only things stored in your browser are the ones needed to keep you signed in and to remember what you typed into the signup form.

Why there is no cookie banner

UK law — the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations — requires consent before storing anything in your browser, with one exception: storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service you have asked for. Signing you in is strictly necessary. Measuring your behaviour is not.

We only do the first, so there is nothing to ask you about. A banner that asked anyway would be theatre.

If that ever changes — if we add analytics, for example — we will ask for your consent properly before setting anything, and update this page.

What is actually stored

We use your browser’s local storage rather than cookies for both of these. Local storage is not sent to our servers with every request the way a cookie is; it stays on your device until it is cleared.

Your sign-in

sb-…-auth-token
Set when you log in, so you stay logged in as you move between pages and when you come back later. It holds the token that identifies your session. Removed when you log out. Set by Supabase, which provides our sign-in system. Strictly necessary.

The signup form

one.pending-onboarding
Holds the business details and preferences you entered while signing up, so they are not lost between confirming your email address and arriving at your account. Deleted as soon as they have been saved to your account. Strictly necessary.

Other sites you are sent to

Two parts of the service hand you over to another company’s page. Once you are there, that company’s own cookies and privacy notice apply, not ours.

  • Stripe — paying, and managing your billing, happens on Stripe’s own checkout and portal pages. Stripe sets cookies there, including for fraud prevention. See stripe.com/privacy.
  • The App Store and Google Play — if you follow a link to an app listing.

We do not embed any third-party scripts, fonts, maps, video players, chat widgets or social buttons on this site. Nothing loads from another company’s server while you read these pages.

Server logs

Separately from anything stored in your browser, our host keeps short-lived request logs that include IP addresses. That is not cookies or tracking — it is how a web server is operated and kept secure. It is covered in our privacy notice.

Clearing or blocking it

You can clear local storage from your browser’s settings, usually under privacy, site data or “clear browsing data”. Blocking it for this site is fine — the pages will still work, but you will not be able to stay signed in.
