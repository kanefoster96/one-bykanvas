Questions.

I get all my work from word of mouth. Why do I need a site?

Word of mouth ends with a Google search. When someone is given your name, they look you up before they ring. If there is nothing there, or an out of date Facebook page, some of them ring the next name on the list. Your site is where the recommendation lands.

I’ve already got a Facebook page.

Keep it. Facebook shows your posts to a fraction of your followers and does not appear when someone searches “plumber Blyth”. Your site does, and it takes the booking while you are on a job.

Yes. That is how most trades use it. The site handles the confirmation and the paperwork.
