1. Who you are contracting with

“Kanvas One” is the service provided by Kane Foster, a sole trader trading as Kanvas One. In these terms “we”, “us” and “Kanvas” mean that business, and “you” means the business taking the plan.

You can reach us at hello@kanvas.one. Our full contact details are on the contact page.

These terms are written for businesses. If you are taking a plan as a consumer rather than for the purposes of a business, see section 8.6, which sets out the cancellation rights you have on top of everything else here.

2. What we provide

On an active plan we design, build, host and look after a website for your business. Depending on your plan that can also include a mobile app, ongoing search work, business email at your own address provided through Google Workspace, and a web address registered and renewed for you. Email runs on Google’s service under Google’s own terms, and like everything else here it is included while your plan is active.

What each plan includes is set out on the plans page and the what’s included page. Those pages form part of these terms. Where a description there conflicts with a clause here, the clause here applies.

We provide the service with reasonable skill and care. We do not promise any particular commercial result — a number of enquiries, a search ranking, sales, or app downloads. Search engines and app stores are run by third parties and neither we nor you control them.

3. Edits, features and priority

After your site is live you can keep asking for changes. There is no monthly allowance and no per-change fee: edits and new features are included on every plan.

  • An edit is a change to existing pages: wording, images, design, prices, structure.
  • A feature is something new that did not exist before: a booking form, a gallery, an extra page with new functionality.
  • The distinction only affects how long the work takes, never what it costs.

Priority. Requests are worked through one at a time for each site, Max requests first, then Business. Within a plan, the request waiting longest goes next. Requests, edits and features are included on every plan; Max buys a place at the front of the queue plus its own extras — monthly search and site improvements, and a business email inbox.

Fair use. Unlimited means you are never charged for asking; it does not mean parallel teams working simultaneously. We do requests in order, at a pace that keeps every customer’s work moving. If a run of requests is large enough that it would crowd out other customers, we may schedule it across a longer period, and we will tell you when to expect what.

Any live tool we set up for you to run yourself — a menu, your prices, a timetable — is always free for you to change instantly, without asking us at all.

We decide in good faith whether a request is an edit or a feature, and how requests are ordered within the rules above. If you disagree, tell us and we will explain the reasoning.

4. Getting your site built

We start the build once we have your completed form. Our ten-working-day estimate runs from that point, and assumes you come back to us on drafts within a couple of working days. It is an estimate, not a deadline: rounds of changes, waiting on your photos or copy, and third parties such as domain registries all move it.

If we cannot start because we are waiting on something from you, the plan still runs and is still billed. We will chase before that happens.

6. Price, payment and VAT

Plans are billed monthly in advance by card through Stripe. The first payment is taken when you sign up and starts the build. There is no setup fee.

Prices are shown in pounds sterling. We are not currently VAT registered, so the price you see is the total price and no VAT is added to it. If we become VAT registered we will tell you before it affects your bill.

Your payment date each month is the anniversary of your first payment. We never see or hold your card number; Stripe does. Their terms and privacy notice cover that part.

From time to time we offer promotional codes. The discount and how long it lasts are as described wherever you were given the code, and are applied by Stripe at checkout. When the discounted period ends, the plan continues at its standard price unless you cancel. A code cannot be exchanged for cash or applied to payments already made.

If a payment fails, Stripe retries it. If it keeps failing we will contact you, and we may suspend the service under section 14 until it is settled.

We may change plan prices. We will give you at least 30 days’ notice by email before a change affects you, and you can cancel under section 8 if you do not want to continue at the new price.

7. Changing plan

You can change plan at any time from your account.

Moving up

An upgrade takes effect immediately. Stripe prorates it: you pay the difference between the two plans for the rest of your current month, not a fresh full month. Your requests move up the queue straight away.

Moving down

A downgrade takes effect at your next payment date. You keep the plan you have paid for until then, and there is no refund for the unused part of the month. Your queue position moves when the new plan starts.

8. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. No further payments are taken after you cancel, and the service runs to the end of the month you have already paid for. Nothing is locked in, and there is no exit fee.

8.1 To cancel, use the billing portal from your account, or email us. Cancelling in Stripe and telling us are equivalent; you do not need to do both.

8.2 When the month you have paid for ends, we take the site offline and stop the plan.

8.3 What you take with you. On request we send you an export of your content and data — the text, images and business information you gave us, and anything your site collected for you, such as enquiries and customer details — and we transfer your web address to you with its transfer code, free of charge (section 9). The site itself — the design, pages, features and code — is part of the service rather than something you buy, so it stops with the plan and is not handed over. Section 11 says more about who owns what.

8.4 Six months to change your mind. We keep your site build and account data for six months after your plan ends. Restart within that time and everything picks up where it left off — the same site, the same features, usually back online within days. If the account stays unused for six months, the build and the data are permanently deleted, and coming back later means starting a fresh build.

8.5 Payments already taken are not refunded, including where you cancel part way through a month.

8.6 If you are a consumer

If you are taking a plan as an individual rather than for business purposes, the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 give you 14 days from signing up to cancel for any reason. Because we start the build as soon as you sign up, you are asking us to begin during that period; if you then cancel within the 14 days, we may charge you a proportionate amount for the work already done. This right is in addition to, not instead of, your right to cancel at any time above.

9. Your web address

Where your plan includes a web address, we register and renew it for you and pay the registry fees for as long as your plan is active.

The web address is yours. If you leave, we will transfer it to you or to a registrar of your choosing, free of charge, provided your account is settled. Ask us and we will start the transfer; registry rules mean it can take a few days, and some registries impose their own waiting periods that we cannot shorten.

If you already own a web address, you keep it and we point it at the site we build. It never passes to us.

If an app is rejected we will tell you why and, where the reason is something we can fix, fix it and resubmit at no extra cost.
